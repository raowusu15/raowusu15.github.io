Research

Computational bioengineering, with a core focus on computer-aided drug discovery and medical imaging.

Research areas

Computational Bioengineering

Computational methods applied to therapeutic discovery, physiological systems and clinical imaging. Covers computer-aided drug discovery, physiological modelling and medical imaging.

Tissue Engineering and Biomaterials

Engineered tissue constructs and biomaterial design, approached from the computational side: in-silico screening for drug delivery and modelling of biomaterial–protein interactions.

Optics and Photonics

Biomedical optics and optical imaging. Optica Amplify Immersion Program scholar; teaching assistant for biomedical optics at KNUST.

Active research

These are the two areas I am currently working in.

Computer-Aided Drug Discovery

Systems pharmacology, structure-based virtual screening and computational toxicology, with an emphasis on natural products from West African medicinal plants.

  • West African Traditional Medicine Systems Pharmacology (WATMSP) platform. Building an open-access platform that integrates phytochemical, molecular, target, ADMET, ethnomedicinal and therapeutic indication data for West African medicinal plants. KCCR · manuscript in preparation
  • Cardiotoxicity prediction and molecular docking. Machine-learning and LLM-assisted workflows evaluating bioactive molecules across hERG, Nav1.5 and Cav1.2 endpoints, with molecular docking and protein–ligand interaction analysis. KCCR · ongoing
  • ACE-targeted antihypertensive drug discovery. Curating and standardising ACE bioactivity records from ChEMBL and PubChem for machine-learning, ligand-based modelling and lead prioritisation. KCCR · ongoing
  • Structure-based virtual screening of NDM-1 inhibitors. A PyRx/AutoDock Vina workflow to screen candidate NDM-1 inhibitors and characterise active-site, metal-coordination and protein–ligand interactions using PyMOL and BIOVIA Discovery Studio. Mar – Apr 2026
  • Molecular modelling and dynamics of KRAS G12D inhibitors. Comparing candidate and reference inhibitors through docking and residue-level interaction profiling, and preparing complexes for molecular dynamics and trajectory-based stability analysis. Jul 2026 – present
  • Training: The InSilico Lab — Computer-Aided Drug Design. Structure-based drug design, molecular docking, ADMET and AI-assisted modelling. Jun – Jul 2026 · certificate

Medical Imaging

Deep learning for medical image segmentation, and the question of whether a model that scores well on a benchmark still holds up when image quality degrades the way it does in a real clinic.

  • Reliability and robustness in breast ultrasound lesion segmentation. Benchmarking U-Net, Attention U-Net, TransUNet and SegFormer-B0 under progressive speckle corruption, with Monte Carlo Dropout for predictive uncertainty and model reliability assessment. Submitted to the MICCAI 2026 MIRASOL Workshop · details
  • Trustworthy deep learning for brain tumour segmentation. Transfer-learning pipelines on the BraTS dataset for multi-modal MRI segmentation, evaluating performance, robustness and predictive uncertainty with clinically relevant metrics. Apr – Jun 2026
  • Training: Spark Academy Imaging Research Program. Deep learning in medical imaging, including a multi-project hackathon. Feb – Aug 2026 · programme
  • Training: IMAGINE Summer School — Imaging without Borders. MRI physics, hardware and analysis. Jun – Aug 2026 · programme

Research

Computational bioengineering, with a core focus on computer-aided drug discovery and medical imaging.

Research areas

Computational Bioengineering

Computational methods applied to therapeutic discovery, physiological systems and clinical imaging. Covers computer-aided drug discovery, physiological modelling and medical imaging.

Tissue Engineering and Biomaterials

Engineered tissue constructs and biomaterial design, approached from the computational side: in-silico screening for drug delivery and modelling of biomaterial–protein interactions.

Optics and Photonics

Exploring optical and photonic methods for biomedical imaging, sensing, spectroscopy, and non-invasive diagnostics, with an interest in how light–tissue interactions can support disease detection and physiological monitoring..

Active research

These are the two areas I am currently working in.

Computer-Aided Drug Discovery & Computational Biology

Computational approaches to therapeutic discovery, with interests in molecular docking, virtual screening, systems pharmacology, drug-safety prediction, protein structure modelling and molecular simulation.

  • West African Traditional Medicine Systems Pharmacology (WATMSP) platform. Leading the development of an AI-enabled systems pharmacology platform integrating phytochemical, molecular, protein-target, disease, ADMET, ethnomedicinal and therapeutic-indication data for West African medicinal plants. GHAiC-K, KCCR · manuscript in preparation
  • Machine-learning cardiotoxicity screening of West African medicinal-plant compounds. Developed and evaluated predictive models for hERG, Nav1.5 and Cav1.2 ion-channel liabilities and applied them to screen phytochemicals for potential cardiac safety risks. GHAiC-K, KCCR · code
  • ACE-targeted antihypertensive drug discovery. Curating and standardising ACE bioactivity records from ChEMBL and PubChem to support machine-learning model development, ligand-based analysis and computational lead prioritisation. GHAiC-K, KCCR · ongoing
  • Validation-gated structure-based screening of Plasmodium falciparum DHFR. Developed a Meeko and AutoDock Vina docking workflow and validated the protocol by reproducing the crystallographic WR99210 binding pose with a heavy-atom RMSD of 1.51 Å before applying it to a nine-compound virtual screen. Jun – Jul 2026 · code
  • AI-based protein structure prediction and structural validation. Predicted human dihydrofolate reductase using AlphaFold3 and evaluated the predicted structure against experimental PDB structure 1U72 using PyMOL, obtaining an RMSD of 0.375 Å across 186 aligned residues. Jul – Aug 2026 · code
  • Molecular dynamics and computational biophysics. Developing practical experience in molecular dynamics simulation using GROMACS, including system preparation, force fields, energy minimisation, equilibration and trajectory analysis for protein–ligand systems. Introductory training · ongoing
  • Training: The InSilico Lab — Computer-Aided Drug Design. Practical training in protein preparation, binding-site prediction, molecular docking, protein–ligand interaction analysis, ADMET assessment and AlphaFold3-based structure evaluation. Jun – Jul 2026 · certificate

Medical Imaging

Deep learning for medical image segmentation, and the question of whether a model that scores well on a benchmark still holds up when image quality degrades the way it does in a real clinic.

  • Reliability and robustness in breast ultrasound lesion segmentation. Benchmarking U-Net, Attention U-Net, TransUNet and SegFormer-B0 under progressive speckle corruption, with Monte Carlo Dropout for predictive uncertainty and model reliability assessment. Submitted to the MICCAI 2026 MIRASOL Workshop · details
  • Training: Spark Academy Imaging Research Program. Deep learning in medical imaging, including a multi-project hackathon. Feb – Aug 2026 · programme
  • Training: IMAGINE Summer School — Imaging without Borders. MRI physics, hardware and analysis. Jun – Aug 2026 · programme
